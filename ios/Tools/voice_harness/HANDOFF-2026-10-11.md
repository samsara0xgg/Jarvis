# Phone voice: state at hand-off (2026-10-11)

Everything below is on `feat/ios-star-chart`, local, unpushed.

## What the voice path does now
- Fresh `AVAudioEngine` per start (the launch-time engine had a permanent 0 Hz input); a configuration change with an unchanged input format restarts in place.
- Engine stop, session deactivation and the configuration-change restart run on `VoiceAudio.sessionQueue`, never the main thread (they block for ~1 s with AirPods). `start()` waits for that queue first.
- Recognition: SpeechTranscriber zh-CN + en-US with `.fastResults`; utterance ends after 0.8 s with no new words and a quiet mic.
- Conversation mode: 10 s follow-up window; 「等一下」 holds listening for 60 s (Mac `conversation_idle_exit_s` / `conversation_wait_s`).
- Words over her: first layer on the phone (`judgeOverHer`: own echo, backchannel, too short, barge). No ducking (yilun). A barge with `ready.judges` holds her voice and sends `say` with `over_her`; follow-ups in conversation mode send `conversation`. The host (ADR 0216) answers `said.verdict`: turn / backchannel / unclear / echo / elsewhere / stop / wait / dismissed / quiet:*. No verdict within 2.5 s: the same `say` again unflagged. Hard stop words act locally at once.
- Words over her that start within 4 s of his last utterance are sent joined to it (a pause had cut his sentence).
- Her caption (`reply`) follows her voice at ~6 speech units/s and paces itself to end with the audio once it has all arrived.
- `PlayerCore` REPORT cursors join end to start, so the host ledger can trust the heard prefix (it logged audible=735 forever before).
